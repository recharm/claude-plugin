# Script structures

Ten hook structures, each with a skeleton, an example to match in rhythm (not in words), and the
footage recipe for each beat. These are rough: bend them to the user's reference video or script,
their product, and the claims the brand can actually make (`website-brand.json`). Write for the ear:
short sentences, ~2.6 words per second including the CTA, URLs and numbers spelled as spoken.

Footage recipes use generic beats; map each to the brand's *live* labels (`filters.json`). Filter
every search with the voice rule from `SKILL.md`. Every ad opens on a hook in the first line and first
~1.5s, and closes on a CTA of ~6-8 words in its own beat.

Placeholders: `[product]` = the confirmed product, `[outcome]` = a result the site or user supports.
Never ship an invented number, review count or timeframe: replace each example figure with a real one
or remove the sentence.

---

## 1. The Testimonial
`HOOK` specific result -> `PROOF` "I was skeptical because..." + context -> `STORY` what happened after
using it -> `CTA` where to get it.

> "My skin hasn't looked like this since I was 22. I've tried literally everything. Serums,
> prescriptions, all of it. A friend told me about [product] and honestly I ignored her for three
> months. Then I tried it. Four weeks later, my dermatologist asked what I'd changed. Link in bio,
> they still have the intro offer."

Footage: one creator, `Actor A-roll` + `Voice` (verified speech), jump cuts, product cutaways where it
is mentioned (`Product` / person using product, B-roll, L-cut off the speaker). Fallback if no verified
speech: VO-read narration over that creator's B-roll, and say so.

## 2. Problem -> Solution
`HOOK` name the problem -> `AGITATION` make it worse -> `SOLUTION` the product as the fix -> `PROOF` one
specific result -> `CTA`.

> "If you're still writing UGC scripts manually, you're wasting hours you don't have. Every brief,
> every hook, every variation, all by hand. That's not a content strategy, that's a second job.
> [Product] generates your full script from your product URL in seconds. We went from 3 hours per
> brief to under 5 minutes. Feel free to try, link below."

Footage: problem half = friction clips (the mess, the frustration, the old way) with hard quick cuts;
pivot = one match cut; solution half = product + result clips with smooth swipes.

## 3. The Comparison
`HOOK` "I tried X products" -> `RANKING` quick, specific dismissal of alternatives -> `WINNER` your
product with a clear reason -> `PROOF` one concrete result -> `CTA`.

> "I tested five AI UGC tools so you don't have to. MakeUGC? Too technical. Arcads? No free trial,
> EUR70 minimum. Creatify? The voiceovers are genuinely painful. HeyGen? Great for translation, not for
> UGC. Reloop was the only one where I got a publish-ready ad without touching a prompt. First video
> took me four minutes."

Footage: categories of alternatives, not rival brand names or readable rival packaging, unless the user
supplies cleared comparison footage; a test/demo clip for the winner. Comparison bars only with the
site's figures. Naming specific competitors in the script is a legal call - flag it to the user.

## 4. The Unboxing / First Impression
`HOOK` "I just got [product] and I need to talk about it" -> `UNBOXING` physical description, first
sensory reactions -> `STANDOUT` one thing that surprised them -> `VERDICT` would they recommend it ->
`CTA`.

> "Okay this just arrived and I wasn't expecting much for this price. The packaging alone... already
> impressed. But this [feature]... I've spent three times more on [competitor product] and it doesn't do
> this. I've had it for twenty minutes and I'm already recommending it. Link in bio."

Footage: Unboxing / opening-package / holding-product scenes; first use close-up.

## 5. Before / After
`HOOK` the "after" first -> `BEFORE` the pain state in detail -> `BRIDGE` what changed (the product) ->
`AFTER` current state, specific and measurable -> `CTA` urgency or scarcity.

> "I'm now creating five UGC ads a day. Three weeks ago I was struggling to finish one. The brief alone
> used to take me two hours. Script, shot list, hooks, all manual. I started using [product] to
> generate everything from my product URL. Now I brief faster, test more hooks, and my ROAS is up 40%.
> They have a free trial, I'll link it."

Footage: result clip first, then the problem, bridge = product in use, then the result again. Rightward
swipes before -> after; visible BEFORE / AFTER labels.

## 6. The Founder Story
`HOOK` unexpected or relatable founding moment -> `PROBLEM` what was missing -> `BUILT IT` "so I built
[product]" -> `TRACTION` social proof -> `CTA` invite the viewer in.

> "I was spending EUR3,000 a month on UGC creators and half the content wasn't even usable. So I built a
> tool to generate it with AI. No prompts, no briefs, no back and forth. Just describe your product and
> get a publish-ready video. We now have [X] brands using it. Free to try."

Only with the **real** story and footage from the user; never present a creator or generated voice as
the founder.

## 7. The Tutorial / How-To
`HOOK` promise a specific outcome in a specific time -> `STEP 1` -> `STEP 2` -> `STEP 3` the result ->
`CTA` "here's how to get started".

> "Here's how I create a full UGC ad in under five minutes. Step one: paste your product URL. Step two:
> tell the agent what vibe you're going for. Step three: review the plan and hit build. That's it. The
> video is generated, captioned, and ready to publish. No editing. No prompts. Link below to try it for
> free."

Footage: one clip per step, in order; slide-in-sequence step labels; rightward swipes.

## 8. The Skeptic
`HOOK` "I didn't believe [bold claim] was possible" -> `DOUBT` voice the audience's exact objection ->
`TRIAL` what made you try it -> `SURPRISE` what happened -> `CTA` "make up your own mind".

> "I thought AI UGC was a gimmick. Avatars that look fake, scripts that sound robotic, nothing you'd
> actually run as an ad. Then someone sent me a video made with [product] and I genuinely couldn't tell
> it was AI. I tried it myself. Three videos in, I'd already replaced two of my monthly creator briefs.
> Make up your own mind. Free trial, link below."

Footage: doubt = the old way / a skeptical beat; trial + surprise = product in use and the result.

## 9. The Day-in-the-Life
`HOOK` "A day in my life with [product]" -> `MORNING` first touchpoint -> `MIDDAY` second context ->
`EVENING` result or ritual -> `CTA` "this is what [outcome] actually looks like".

> "Morning routine has completely changed since I started using [product]. I used to spend the first
> hour of my day writing ad briefs. Now I generate three scripts while my coffee brews. By noon I've
> already sent creative to my team. By the end of day I've reviewed performance on yesterday's tests.
> This is what scaling creative actually looks like. Link below."

Footage: lifestyle b-roll by time of day (morning/midday/evening), the product in each context.

## 10. The Urgent CTA
`HOOK` the offer or deadline, immediately -> `REASON` why now -> `PROOF` one fast social-proof signal ->
`CTA` extremely direct and specific.

> "The launch pricing for [product] ends Friday. After that it's going to [higher price]. We've had [X]
> sign-ups in the first week, the free trial is still live but I don't know for how long. If you've been
> thinking about it, now is the time. Link below, takes thirty seconds to start."

Only with an offer, deadline and numbers the user confirms are real and live today. Footage: product
shot + proof clip + an end card carrying the offer.

---

## Custom, mixed and VO/VSL

- **Custom**: take the closest structure and say which beats you changed.
- **Mix of types**: ask which parts come from which structure (e.g. Skeptic hook + Tutorial middle +
  Urgent CTA) and build one continuous script, not two stitched ads.
- **Motion graphics**: one idea per scene, 2.5-4s each; see `motion-graphics.md`.
- **VO / VSL**: B-roll and No Actor only, clip audio muted; longer scripts keep ~2.6 words/s.
- **Reference video or script from the user**: it outranks these defaults. Match its pacing, hook
  style, caption look and transition grammar, and say what you took from it.
